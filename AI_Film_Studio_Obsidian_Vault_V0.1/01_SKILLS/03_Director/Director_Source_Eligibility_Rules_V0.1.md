# Director Source Eligibility Rules V0.1

## Evidence tiers

| Tier | Eligible source | Formal use |
|---|---|---|
| A | Director / educator’s official book, official course, official long-form interview, Guild, Academy, or film-institute material | Preferred primary source for formal distillation once access and quotation terms are confirmed |
| B | Credible institution’s complete interview, directing lecture, or transcript with visible provenance | Permitted supplementary formal source when method is extractable |
| C | High-quality secondary analysis or catalogue | Discovery only; may identify an A/B source but cannot establish a formal method claim |
| D | Blog summary, quote collection, short clip, anonymous transcript, AI-generated summary, or untraceable social content | Prohibited as formal method evidence |

## Per-source audit gate

Before a source may enter formal distillation, record all of the following:

1. Source exists at a stable, traceable publisher / owner URL.
2. Source is accessible under the available licence, purchase, library, membership, or permission route.
3. The accessible body is substantial enough for method extraction; a landing page, clip title, or sales blurb alone is not enough.
4. The source identifies its speaker, event / edition, publisher, and provenance.
5. It yields specific directing-method evidence rather than celebrity commentary alone.
6. Quotation, reproduction, and attribution boundaries are understood and respected.
7. Its contribution and overlap are mapped to one or more provisional domains.

## Current source-policy findings

- DGA’s public programme identifies peer-to-peer craft interviews and searchable public highlights; DGA also requires permission to quote or reproduce interview material. Treat that permission boundary as binding. [DGA Visual History](https://www.dga.org/craft/visualhistory) · [usage terms](https://www.dga.org/Craft/VisualHistory/Usage)
- A paid course or eBook can be Tier A only after the Studio has lawful access to its substantive body. Its public outline can support candidate discovery, not formal method extraction.
- The planned work will synthesize only short paraphrased method findings with source traces; it will not reproduce course videos, books, or interview transcripts.

## Explicit exclusions

No quote collection, short motivational clip, AI summary, unlicensed scan, or cinematography-only material may be promoted into the Director’s formal evidence base without independently meeting this gate.
