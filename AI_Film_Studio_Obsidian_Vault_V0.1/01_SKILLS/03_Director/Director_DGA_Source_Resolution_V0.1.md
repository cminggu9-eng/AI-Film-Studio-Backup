## Non-promoted DGA material

- DGA Visual History remains a discovery pool, not a Phase 2 source body: topic-indexed highlights or a collection listing do not by themselves pass the source-body gate.
- DGA short highlights (including Podeswa / Coleman) remain supplemental leads only; their short duration is insufficient for formal source distillation.
- DGA Q&A video titles without a specific relevant segment are no longer treated as a generic source pool.

## Rights and use boundary

Use the DGA bodies through lawful public access, source attribution, concise paraphrase, and the project’s source-trace ledger. Do not reproduce source text or treat the selection forecast as extracted method.
