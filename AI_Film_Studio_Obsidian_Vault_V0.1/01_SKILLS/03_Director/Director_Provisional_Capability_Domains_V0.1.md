# Director Provisional Capability Domains V0.1

These domains are a **DISCOVERY FRAME**, not a final capability model. Source evidence may merge, split, rename, remove, or add domains only with a traceable rationale.

| ID | Provisional domain | Discovery question |
|---|---|---|
| DR-C01 | Scene Intent Preservation | Does the staging preserve the legally established scene intent and outcome? |
| DR-C02 | Visual Storytelling | What can action, image, sound, and duration communicate without redundant explanation? |
| DR-C03 | Blocking / Staging | How do bodies, objects, entrances, exits, and movement make the action playable? |
| DR-C04 | Spatial Geography | What must the audience understand about position, distance, access, and change? |
| DR-C05 | Audience Attention | What should the audience notice, defer, or reassess now? |
| DR-C06 | Reveal / Conceal | How is information timed without altering the underlying story? |
| DR-C07 | Shot Purpose | What dramatic / audience function justifies a shot? |
| DR-C08 | Coverage Logic | What coverage is necessary for performance, information, editability, and alternatives? |
| DR-C09 | Camera Position Logic | Why is the audience placed here rather than elsewhere? |
| DR-C10 | Camera Movement Motivation | What change or relation motivates movement? |
| DR-C11 | Composition / Dramatic Emphasis | How does arrangement create emphasis while preserving clarity? |
| DR-C12 | Visual Rhythm / Pacing | How do duration, cuts, action, silence, and reaction regulate experience? |
| DR-C13 | Screen Direction / Spatial Continuity | How are screen direction and evolving geography made legible? |
| DR-C14 | Scene Entry / Exit Visual Treatment | How do visual entrance and departure serve the existing scene transition? |
| DR-C15 | Transition Logic | What carries the audience across scene boundaries without rewriting story logic? |
| DR-C16 | Character / Camera Relationship | When does camera relation help audience access without inventing performance psychology? |
| DR-C17 | Actor Direction Boundary | What staging / communication need belongs to Director versus Character & Acting? |
| DR-C18 | Art Direction Handoff Boundary | What spatial / visual need should be handed to Art rather than designed here? |
| DR-C19 | Production Practicality | What production burden, contingency, or collaboration constraint changes the staging choice? |
| DR-C20 | AI / External Production Constraint Boundary | What future constraint interface may be received without inventing AI-directing rules? |
