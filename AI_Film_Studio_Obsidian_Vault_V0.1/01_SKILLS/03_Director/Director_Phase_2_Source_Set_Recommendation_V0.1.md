# Director Phase 2 Source Set Recommendation V0.1

This is a source-set recommendation, not authorization to read, distill, or synthesize methods.

## Core — four available source bodies

| Source body | Forecast contribution | Why core |
|---|---|---|
| DGA-DR-01 Jeff Nichols / Adam Stone — *Collaborators* | Blocking / staging through point of view; camera movement motivation; Director–DP–Production Designer collaboration boundary | Closes DR-C10 and establishes the minimum DR-C18 handoff account without adopting DP or Art execution authority |
| DGA-DR-02 Vince Gilligan — *Shot to Remember: Breaking Bad* | Actor geography, eyelines, relation in frame, audience orientation, coverage as spatial clarity | Closes DR-C13 with a director-native intimate-scene case |
| DGA-DR-03 Lesli Linka Glatter — *The Good Fight* | Scene intent, turns, transition scenes, visual scale, planning under episodic time constraints | Closes DR-C15 without importing editor-only cutting mechanics |
| DGA-DR-04 Clint Eastwood — *Constructing a Film* | Geography-first coverage and connective scene space | Supplements / stress-tests DR-C13 in a wider / ensemble construction context |

The core count is **four** source bodies: within the Phase 2 target of four to six, with distinct primary contributions.

## Supplemental — targeted triangulation only

- Sundance *Conversations with Production Designers*: DR-C18 from the Production Designer side; no Art Director system extraction.
- Sundance *Building the World of Your Film*: Director-facing visual-intent communication across departments.
- DGA Visual History only after a specific, substantive interview body is individually audited.

## Reserve

- DGA short highlights, including Podeswa and Coleman, may guide later source discovery but cannot be a standalone formal source body.
- Sundance / DGA material that becomes relevant only after a recorded Phase 2 gap should be added as a targeted supplemental source, not as a broad candidate expansion.

## Excluded

- Exact title “Introduction to Directing” until a stable official source body is identified.
- Third-party archives, quote collections, unlicensed scans, AI-generated summaries, and camera / cinematography manuals used as Director authority.
- Any source that would grant Director story, acting-method, production-design-system, full-editorial, or continuity authority.
