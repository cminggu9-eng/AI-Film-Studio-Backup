## Matrix interpretation

- No candidate is treated as a complete Director model.
- Belli / Rooney and Weston are complementary but overlap strongly on preparation, blocking, and actor work; their later source selection must be selective.
- `DR-C10`, `DR-C13`, `DR-C15`, and `DR-C18` lack confirmed strong Tier A body evidence. `DR-C20` is intentionally unfilled: it remains a future interface boundary, not an AI-directing rule.
