# Director Studio-Native Rules Draft V0.1

## Status and use

These are traceable **draft classifications** for Phase 4 review. They are not a Capability Model, Production Skill, runtime contract, shot-list template, or final operating rule set. They answer only: `HOW SHOULD THIS ASSIGNED SCENE BE STAGED, SEEN, HEARD, AND EXPERIENCED?`

| Rule ID | Draft rule | Classification | Originating DR-D methods | Relationship IDs | DR-C mapping | Boundary note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DR-SNR-D01 | Preserve locked Story Intent, Scene Function, material dramatic event, and outcome. A Director visual choice may not change them for spectacle, transition, composition, performance space, or efficiency. | HARD | D01, D09 | R01, R06 | C01, C02, C05, C07 | Showrunner / Scene Writer authority remains upstream; conflict returns upstream. |
| DR-SNR-D02 | State the scene/audience purpose that justifies a proposed visual choice; reject display whose only account is that it looks cinematic. | DEFAULT | D01, D03, D09, D16 | R01, R02 | C05, C07, C10, C12 | Director supplies purpose; DP retains technical means. |
| DR-SNR-D03 | Keep position, relation, access, and room geography legible by default. Purposeful reorientation is permitted only when it has an explicit scene/audience account. | DEFAULT | D02, D06, D13 | R03, R04 | C03, C04, C08, C13, C16 | Director specifies audience understanding; no fixed coverage grammar or Continuity takeover. |
| DR-SNR-D04 | Use position, composition, or concealment to time information only when the scene relation explains what the audience should notice, defer, or discover. | CONDITIONAL | D07, D08 | R05 | C05, C06, C09, C11 | Director sets information need; Art/DP implement feasibility; does not change locked facts. |
| DR-SNR-D05 | Camera movement or visual flourish requires a specific change in story state, audience attention, character relation, or spatial information; never use it as a default decoration. | CONDITIONAL | D03, D16 | R02 | C05, C07, C10, C12 | No DP technical manual and no anti-static-camera rule. |
| DR-SNR-D06 | Let coverage answer performance, information, spatial relation, and necessary alternatives—not a master/medium/close-up template. Leave a performer only when the scene supplies a reason. | DEFAULT | D05, D06, D11, D12, D13, D14 | R03, R04, R08 | C07, C08, C12, C13, C16, C17 | Director protects dramatic access; Editor retains full post methodology; Character & Acting retains acting method. |
| DR-SNR-D07 | When a scene's assigned event is performance-carried, stage and cover it to protect playable turns and discovery. Restraint, sparse coverage, dense coverage, or a long take are all valid when purpose-supported. | CONDITIONAL | D05, D11, D14 | R08, R11 | C03, C07, C08, C12, C16, C17 | Director-facing staging only; no emotional scale, micro-expression, or actor-training system. |
| DR-SNR-D08 | Classify the assigned scene's turns, emotional weight, and transition function before choosing visual scale and required material. A transition is a Director-facing scene-function condition, not an editorial method. | CONDITIONAL | D09, D10, D12 | R06, R07, R12 | C01, C05, C12, C15, C19 | Scene Writer function stays locked; Editor retains edit/post authority. |
| DR-SNR-D09 | Prepare enough spatial/staging intent to make required material executable; then accept an on-set proposal only when it passes the same scene-purpose and spatial-need test. | CONDITIONAL | D04, D07, D12, D15 | R09, R10 | C03, C04, C08, C18, C19 | Director names needs and decides relevance; DP/Art/actors contribute without Director micromanagement. |
| DR-SNR-D10 | Evaluate location, complexity, scale, and coverage burden against required dramatic function. Efficiency cannot delete a required beat; burden cannot be escalated without demonstrated scene value. | CONDITIONAL | D04, D10, D12, D15 | R07, R09, R11, R12 | C03, C04, C05, C12, C18, C19 | Production owns cost/schedule solution; Art/DP own means; Director does not change story to save effort. |

## Classification audit

| Classification | Count | Qualification |
|---|---:|---|
| HARD | 1 | D01 + D09, reinforced by Charter boundary; directly prevents upstream authority loss and a locked-event violation. |
| DEFAULT | 3 | D02, D03, D06: repeatedly source-supported, broadly applicable, and explicitly exception-capable. |
| CONDITIONAL | 6 | D04, D05, D07–D10: application depends on intent, information state, scene type, performance need, or practical condition. |
| OPTIONAL | 0 | No frozen evidence warrants an optional rule solely to represent personal style. |

## Explicit exclusions

- No rule for `DR-C14`: `NO EVIDENCE / NOT DISTILLABLE`.
- No rule for `DR-C20`: `DEFERRED / FUTURE EXTERNAL PRODUCTION CONSTRAINT INTERFACE`.
- No rule defines lens, lighting, rig, camera setting, editing grammar, acting technique, design system, continuity system, AI motion constraint, or generation workflow.
