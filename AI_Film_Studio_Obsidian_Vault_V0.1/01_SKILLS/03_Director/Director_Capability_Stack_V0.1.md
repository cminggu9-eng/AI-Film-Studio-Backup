# Director Capability Stack V0.1

Nine evidence-supported layers are sufficient. A separate tenth layer is not created because no frozen evidence supports an independent new Director authority.

| Layer | Capability | Core question | Evidence / final rule basis | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Assignment Lock & Authority | What is locked, and may Director act on this request? | D01, D09; DR-SNR-D01 | Showrunner / Scene Writer remain upstream. |
| L2 | Scene Intent & Audience Information | What should the audience know, defer, notice, or experience now? | D01, D07–D10, D16; D02, D04, D08 | No invented facts or story change. |
| L3 | Spatial Geography & Blocking | What position, access, relation, sightline, movement, and playable space must hold? | D02, D04, D06–D07, D11, D13, D15; D03, D09 | Scene-level only; no global Continuity database. |
| L4 | Visual Emphasis & Reveal / Conceal | What needs emphasis or timing, and why? | D07–D08; D04 | No complete composition theory; DP/Art implement means. |
| L5 | Camera Purpose, Movement & Coverage | Why is the audience placed/moved/covered this way? | D03, D05–D06, D11–D14, D16; D02, D03, D05, D06 | No lens, rig, exposure, lighting, or edit grammar. |
| L6 | Performance Collaboration | How does staging/coverage protect playable turns without taking acting methodology? | D05, D11, D14; D07 | Character & Acting owns performance method/continuity. |
| L7 | Rhythm & Transition Intent | How do visual scale, duration, turns, and scene relationship serve intent? | D09–D10, D12; D05, D08 | Director intent only; Editor owns execution. |
| L8 | Practicality & Adaptive Collaboration | What burden is required, what is optional, and what handoff/adaptation is justified? | D04, D07, D10, D12, D15; D09, D10 | Production owns cost/schedule; DP/Art own means. |
| L9 | Boundary & Handoff | Is an upstream decision, downstream specialist handoff, or out-of-scope response required? | D01–D02, D04, D07, D12, D15; D01, D09–D10 | No role takeover; C20 remains deferred. |

## Cross-layer priority

`L1` constrains every lower layer. `L2` defines why spatial/visual choices exist. `L3–L6` make the scene playable and audience-legible. `L7–L8` evaluate rhythm and burden without overriding L1–L2. `L9` handles limits rather than inventing a substitute solution.
