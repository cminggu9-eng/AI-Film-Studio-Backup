# Director Anti-Mechanical Directing Taxonomy V0.1

This taxonomy names only failure patterns supported by `DR-D01–DR-D16` and the Phase 3 relationships. It is not a cinematography error catalogue.

| Taxonomy ID | Anti-mechanical failure | Evidence basis | Detection question | Purpose-supported exception |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AM-D01 | Visual flourish overrides assigned scene intent | D01, D09, D16 / R01, R02 | Does the choice preserve the locked material dramatic event and audience purpose? | A conspicuous choice may remain when it has an explicit scene/audience account. |
| AM-D02 | Camera movement without purpose | D03, D16 / R02 | What state, relation, attention, or spatial information change requires this movement? | Static, handheld, dolly, or any movement form is valid when purpose-supported. |
| AM-D03 | Coverage-template repetition | D06, D11, D12, D13, D14 / R03, R04, R08 | Is this coverage present because of performance, information, relation, or required alternative—or only habit? | Sparse or dense coverage is valid when needed. |
| AM-D04 | Geography-breaking without audience account | D02, D06, D13 / R03, R04 | Can the audience still understand the relevant position, relation, access, or intended disorientation? | Deliberate confusion is valid if its scene/audience purpose is explicit. |
| AM-D05 | Arbitrary reveal/conceal | D07, D08 / R05 | What is the audience to notice, defer, or discover, and why now? | A simple or overt reveal is valid if it serves the locked information relationship. |
| AM-D06 | Fragmentation that abandons a performance-carried event | D05, D11, D14 / R08, R11 | Is a cutaway or coverage change interrupting the playable turn without a scene reason? | Fragmented coverage is valid when a story/spatial/information reason exists. |
| AM-D07 | Frozen preparation that rejects relevant discovery | D07, D15 / R10 | Does the plan prevent a better on-set proposal that preserves/improves scene purpose? | A plan may remain unchanged when later proposals do not pass the same test. |
| AM-D08 | Complexity without dramatic benefit | D04, D10, D12 / R07, R12 | What required scene function justifies the added location, scale, or coverage burden? | Complex work is valid when the value is demonstrated. |
| AM-D09 | Efficiency deletes required dramatic material | D10, D12 / R07, R12 | Has time/cost pressure removed a required beat, turn, or information need? | Economy is valid when required function remains protected. |
| AM-D10 | Blocking for camera convenience alone | D02, D11, D15 / R04, R08, R10 | Does the placement preserve character relation, playable turns, and scene purpose? | Camera-aware blocking is valid when it also satisfies those requirements. |

## Boundary

The taxonomy diagnoses a Director decision's purpose account. It does not diagnose actor technique, DP craft settings, editing execution, Art design, continuity records, or AI-production constraints.
