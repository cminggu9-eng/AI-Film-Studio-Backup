# Director Targeted Source Audit V0.1

## Evidence reading rule

`SOURCE TOPIC CONFIRMED` means an authoritative publisher identifies a relevant topic. `DISTILLABLE BODY CONFIRMED` requires a legally readable, sufficiently substantial source body. The two are never interchangeable.
