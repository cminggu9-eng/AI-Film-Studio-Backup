# Director Phase 1B — Targeted Gap Discovery Plan V0.1

## Scope

This plan continues, and does not redesign, the Phase 1 Charter, DR-C01–DR-C20 discovery framework, candidate audit, coverage matrix, role boundaries, or RISK-01–10.

The only target gaps are:

| Domain | Discovery question | Minimum Phase 2 readiness |
|---|---|---|
| DR-C10 | Is camera movement motivated by story, character, information, or audience experience rather than technical display? | Moderate |
| DR-C13 | How does direction establish and preserve audience orientation, actor geography, eyelines, and purposeful spatial disruption? | Moderate |
| DR-C15 | How does scene-to-scene transition serve visual, spatial, action, information, and dramatic flow without taking over editing methodology? | Moderate |
| DR-C18 | What visual / spatial intent does Director communicate and delegate while Production Design retains design-system authority? | Moderate |
| DR-C20 | Future external production constraint interface only | `DEFERRED` |

## Discovery protocol

1. Audit authoritative source **bodies**, not merely titles or course outlines.
2. Mark source topic confirmation separately from body availability and evidence strength.
3. Resolve DGA into no more than four exact Director / source-body candidates.
4. Use Director–DP discussion only for audience / story purpose; do not convert cinematography parameters into Director authority.
5. Use production-design material only to define handoff and collaboration boundary; do not develop an Art Director skill.
6. Use transition material only for scene storytelling; do not absorb editing mechanics or post-production authority.
7. Do not start source distillation, method extraction, cross-distillation, Studio-Native Rules, Capability Model, Production Skill, Runtime, Executor, or provider work.
