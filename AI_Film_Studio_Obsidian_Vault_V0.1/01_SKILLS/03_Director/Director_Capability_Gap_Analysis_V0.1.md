# Director Capability Gap Analysis V0.1

## Candidate-supported areas

The current candidate set has credible **potential** coverage for scene analysis, visual story, blocking, actor collaboration, shot / coverage reasoning, and practical preparation. This is source-availability evidence only; it is not a confirmed capability finding until lawful source bodies are independently distilled.

## Repetition to control

- Belli / Rooney, Weston, and Sundance all cover scene preparation, blocking, and actor work. Do not distill all three as interchangeable general-directing sources.
- DGA case interviews should contribute case-grounded visual / information decisions, rather than duplicate a generic camera vocabulary.

## Material gaps

| Gap | Status | Required next evidence |
|---|---|---|
| DR-C10 Camera Movement Motivation | No strong confirmed source body | Identify an A/B source that explains motivated movement as scene / audience logic, not as cinematography style alone |
| DR-C13 Screen Direction / Spatial Continuity | No strong confirmed source body | Identify a directing source with location / spatial clarity and editability evidence; avoid handing continuity authority to Director |
| DR-C15 Transition Logic | Weak / case-specific only | Seek source material on visual scene transition and information carryover, while preserving Showrunner / Scene Writer authority |
| DR-C18 Art Direction Handoff | Moderate at best | Seek concrete collaboration evidence that preserves Art Director ownership rather than visual-design takeover |
| Practical source access | Unresolved | Decide whether the Studio may purchase / borrow / enrol in the lawful full bodies for the Belli / Rooney, Weston, and Sundance core sources |
| DR-C20 AI external constraint | Intentionally deferred | Keep only an interface boundary until external production capability is separately defined |

## Role-boundary findings

- `DR-C01` blocks any rewrite of premise, arc, Canon, or Scene Writer’s scene function.
- `DR-C17` captures communication and staging needs only; acting methodology stays with Character & Acting.
- `DR-C18` accepts visual / spatial requirements and production-design needs, but no Art-system, look, palette, or Canon.
- Spatial clarity supports continuity; it does not transfer cross-scene continuity authority to Director.

## Gap conclusion

Discovery has identified an economical, complementary candidate set, but three core sources remain access-gated and four directing domains lack strong confirmed source bodies. The Phase 1 recommendation is therefore **not ready** pending a user source-access decision; no phase transition is automatic.
