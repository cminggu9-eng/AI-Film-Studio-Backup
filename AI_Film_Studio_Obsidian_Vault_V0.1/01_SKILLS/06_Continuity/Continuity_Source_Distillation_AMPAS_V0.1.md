## D. Source scope

The source explains how screen relations and editorial presentation influence viewer comprehension. It does not authorize Continuity to select shots, prescribe cuts, construct montage, set pace, or rewrite the story.

## G. Rejected candidate methods

Editorial pace, structure, shot selection, and shot combination are editor/director activity (p.2, lines 194-201). Classroom shot lists and cut analyses are not Continuity-native operational methods (p.3, lines 248-257). Kuleshov/montage construction, order changes, compression, transitions, camera-specific treatment, and splice mechanics are likewise editorial techniques, not a Continuity authority. Viewer-assumed connection never proves a story fact or character knowledge state.
