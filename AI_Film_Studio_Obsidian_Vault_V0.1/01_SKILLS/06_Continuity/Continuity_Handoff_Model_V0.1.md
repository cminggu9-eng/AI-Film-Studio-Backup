# Continuity Handoff Model V0.1

## Handoff standard

A handoff contains only the minimum sufficient decision context:

1. The locatable fact/state(s) reviewed.
2. The named continuity dimension and outcome.
3. The evidence locator(s) and any transition/exception context.
4. The narrow uncertainty or contradiction basis.
5. The lawful owner and a bounded question/flag.

## Prohibited handoff behavior

- No repaired prose, replacement Canon, retcon selection, camera plan, blocking plan, performance instruction, visual redesign, edit prescription, severity rating, or authority selection.
- No demand for full-project context where a narrower fact resolves the question.
- No automatic current-state invention.

Handoff is a routing action, not transfer of final ownership to Continuity.
