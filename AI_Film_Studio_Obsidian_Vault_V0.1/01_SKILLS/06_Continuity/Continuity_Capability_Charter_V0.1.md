# Continuity Capability Charter V0.1

## Studio-specific mission

Continuity is the studio's **cross-department state check**. It observes, compares, records, flags and routes evidence about what is already true, what state is current, what has lawfully changed, and what may contradict an authorized source across scenes, sequences, episodes and the story.

It is not a generic script-supervisor checklist and not a Canon encyclopedia. Its concern is the continuity of authorized facts, states, knowledge, objects, visual conditions, performance-relevant conditions, time, space and cause-to-effect relationships.

## Core responsibility

- Preserve traceability from an observed continuity concern to the relevant authorized evidence.
- Test whether a changed state is explicit, lawfully implied, authorized off-screen, explained by time passage, or unresolved.
- Distinguish an apparent contradiction from ambiguity, a character belief, a lie, a perspective limit, a nonlinear presentation, or an authorized discontinuity.
- Flag and hand off a concern to its lawful owner. Continuity does not make the corrective creative decision.

## Owns

- Continuity observation, comparison, evidence citation and issue classification.
- State-memory questions covering fact, time, knowledge, relationship, object, appearance, physical condition, environment, performance-relevant carry and spatial/action facts.
- Cross-scene state-transition checks, contradiction flags, uncertainty annotations and ownership routing.
- A future-facing state-snapshot *logic boundary* only; no database, schema, tracker or runtime implementation in this phase.

## Does not own

- Canon, premise, retcons, world rules, structural outcomes, or any source-of-truth precedence decision.
- Scene rewriting, dialogue rewriting, dramatic turns, action redesign, staging, blocking, camera, coverage, performance direction, visual design, language polishing, schedule, call sheets or on-set paperwork workflow.
- A requirement that all scenes look or feel the same. A difference is lawful when it is authorized, evidenced, explained, intentionally discontinuous, or still unresolved.

## Operating question

> What was already true; what is true now; what changed; is the change legally evidenced; and who must decide if the evidence conflicts?

## Phase-1 limitation

This charter is a discovery framing only. It creates no final Continuity rules, authority precedence, severity labels, state fields, tracker, database, Skill, Runtime, Executor, provider call or production workflow.
