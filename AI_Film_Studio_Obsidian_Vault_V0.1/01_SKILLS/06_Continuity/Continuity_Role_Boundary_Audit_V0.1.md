# Continuity Role Boundary Audit V0.1

| Role | Lawful owner | Continuity may | Continuity must not | Handoff trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Showrunner | Canon, premise, world rules, arcs, outcomes, retcons and structural decisions | Detect, locate and cite a Canon conflict or possible unauthorized retcon | Change Canon, resolve a retcon, add lore or decide precedence | Locked fact or Canon source conflicts. |
| Scene Writer | Scene construction, dialogue, action, beats, turn and outcome | Test facts, knowledge, time, prop and action-result continuity | Rewrite, redirect or repair scene prose | A scene's authored fact/action conflicts with authorized evidence. |
| Director | Staging, blocking, camera, coverage and audience/directorial execution | Preserve position, entry/exit and screen-direction implications as state evidence | Stage, block, select camera/coverage or tell the Director how to fix it | Proposed/observed staging makes state facts incompatible. |
| Character & Acting | Playable intention, reaction, body, voice and performance choice | Track evidenced fatigue, tension, injury influence and relationship carry | Give acting directions or impose an emotional line | A performance-relevant state conflicts with a prior/authorized state. |
| Art Director | Visual world, costume/appearance design, props, environment and visual design logic | Track possession, condition, dirt, damage, wear and visual progression | Redesign costume, props, set or visual world | Visual state evidence conflicts across scenes. |
| Shared QA | Language, textual QA and user-facing language consistency | Flag fact/name/date/reference inconsistency as continuity evidence | Polish language, translate or judge prose quality | The text is linguistically sound but contains a continuity concern. |
| Production / script-supervision practice | On-set execution, paperwork, schedule and physical production process | Extract transferable observation/recording judgment later | Import slate, lens sheets, camera rolls, takes, call sheets or physical forms as Studio requirements | A future production interface is separately authorized. |

## Boundary conclusions

- Continuity is a **cross-department state check**, never a cross-department boss.
- A flag contains evidence, scope, uncertainty and owner routing; the lawful owner decides and performs the correction.
- A real-world script supervisor's on-set paperwork is useful only as a source of transferable judgment. It does not authorize a tracker, database or Runtime design.
- “Screen direction/action” remains Director territory except for the minimum factual state needed to identify an incompatible transition.
