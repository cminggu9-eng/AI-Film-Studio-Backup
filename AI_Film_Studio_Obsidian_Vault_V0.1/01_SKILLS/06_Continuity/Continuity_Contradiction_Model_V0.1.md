# Continuity Contradiction Model V0.1

## Classification model

| Classification | Conditions | Continuity action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CONTINUITY PRESERVED | Relevant supplied evidence is materially compatible. | Preserve; do not manufacture a concern. |
| AUTHORIZED OR SUPPORTED CHANGE | Traceable change basis exists. | Annotate/allow; do not force old state back. |
| INTENTIONAL DISCONTINUITY | Specific formal/context evidence supports the difference. | Preserve exception; do not repair or authorize new deviation. |
| MISSING CONTEXT | Essential fact/state is absent. | Request minimum essential context or route question. |
| AMBIGUITY | More than one state/reading remains lawful. | Mark uncertainty; do not select fact. |
| BELIEF / PERSPECTIVE DIFFERENCE | Claim/belief/audience exposure differs from story truth or character knowledge. | Keep planes separate; flag only actual evidence conflict. |
| POTENTIAL CONTRADICTION | Possible incompatible evidence remains unresolved after checks. | Cite both sides, scope, unknown/exception status, and owner. |
| CONFIRMED CONTRADICTION | Two applicable locatable evidence items establish incompatible state for same context; no supported change/exception/context removes conflict. | Flag and route; never resolve, retcon, or rewrite. |

## Verification order

Evidence locator → same entity/context check → temporal/order check → causal/transition-basis check → knowledge-plane check → intentional-discontinuity check → missing/ambiguity check → classification → owner routing.

## Materiality boundary

Continuity may use a qualitative materiality check: assess whether a difference can affect Canon, causality, comprehension, physical possibility, knowledge legality, scene legality, visual/performance continuity, or cross-department consistency. It must not create Critical/Major/Minor tiers, priority scores, or a fixed severity taxonomy. Severity taxonomy: NOT EVIDENCE-SUPPORTED.

Retcon methodology remains boundary-only: detect retcon-like conflict, cite it, and hand off to Showrunner/upstream authority.
