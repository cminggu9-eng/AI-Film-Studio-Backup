# Continuity Capability Stack V0.1

The nine layers below derive from CT-D methods and CT-R01–CT-R14; they are not a mechanical adoption of the earlier ten-layer example.

| Layer | Capability | Final rules / CT-D contribution | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L1 | Evidence and fact foundation | CT-R01; D01/D04/D08/D10 | Only locatable supplied evidence; no fact invention. |
| L2 | Relevant state and identity dimensions | CT-R06, CT-R07, CT-R13; D01/D02/D04/D09/D11 | Track named decision-relevant dimensions, not every detail. |
| L3 | Time, knowledge, causal, and spatial context | CT-R03, CT-R05, CT-R08, CT-R09; D05/D06/D10/D12-D16 | No unseen chronology, knowledge engine, simulation, camera, or blocking. |
| L4 | Current-to-new state comparison | CT-R06–CT-R09; D02/D05/D06/D14 | Compare evidenced state only; no sameness mandate. |
| L5 | Legitimate change and formal exception check | CT-R04, CT-R10–CT-R12; D07/D12/D13/D15/D17 | No invented off-screen event, automatic exception, or authorization engine. |
| L6 | Contradiction and uncertainty outcome | CT-R01, CT-R02, CT-R12, CT-R14; D03/D10/D15/D17 | No severity taxonomy, truth selection, or repair. |
| L7 | Materiality and significance filter | CT-R06, CT-R13; D01/D02/D04 | Consider only decision-relevant effect; no scoring system or bookkeeping. |
| L8 | Authority, owner routing, and handoff | CT-R02, CT-R14; D03/D04 | Continuity is a checker/router, never owner or precedence engine. |
| L9 | Preserved current-state reference | CT-R13; D01/D04 | Capability-level comparison baseline only; no storage design. |
