| Question | Phase-1 evidence result | Gap / required later handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical, appearance, prop and temporal continuity | ScreenSkills provides a strong public practitioner checklist. | Can be a future verified CT-D input only after a separate authorized Nuwa call. |

## Blocking conclusion

The discovery set is sufficient to define a disciplined Phase-2 plan, but not sufficient to authorize a robust 4–6 body formal distillation set. The block is **source-body availability and missing methodology**, not a lack of role intent.

No remediation is started in Phase 1. No source is silently substituted, and no limited/paid/preview material is promoted to formal evidence.
