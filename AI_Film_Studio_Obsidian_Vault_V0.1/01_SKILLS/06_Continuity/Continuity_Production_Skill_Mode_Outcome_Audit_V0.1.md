## Capability outcomes

The six Phase 4 primary outcomes remain exact, mutually exclusive capability-level vocabulary:

1. CONTINUITY PRESERVED
2. AUTHORIZED OR SUPPORTED CHANGE
3. INTENTIONAL DISCONTINUITY
4. CONTEXT INSUFFICIENT
5. POTENTIAL CONTRADICTION
6. CONFIRMED CONTRADICTION

The staging Skill preserves precedence: CONTEXT INSUFFICIENT prevents CONFIRMED CONTRADICTION; INTENTIONAL DISCONTINUITY and AUTHORIZED OR SUPPORTED CHANGE are evaluated before contradiction. Handoff remains separate from the primary outcome.

| Mutual exclusivity preserved | PASS |
| Precedence preserved | PASS |

## Handoff

Phase 4 defines a handoff standard and owner routing, not formal named handoff labels. The staging Skill keeps this absence:

- no formal handoff label was invented;
- handoff contains only minimum sufficient evidence/context, named dimension/outcome, owner, and bounded question/flag;
- handoff never contains a repair, replacement Canon, retcon selection, staging/camera/performance/design/edit prescription, severity rating, or authority selection;
- handoff is routing, not transfer of final ownership to Continuity.
