# Continuity Ownership Routing Model V0.1

| Concern under review | Route | Continuity role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Canon, world, arc, locked fact, retcon-like conflict | Showrunner / upstream story authority | Detect, cite, classify, hand off. |
| Scene content, story action, dialogue fact | Scene Writer | Locate conflict/evidence; do not rewrite. |
| Staging, blocking, camera execution, coverage | Director | Flag factual spatial/action relation only. |
| Cut, shot choice, montage construction, on-set technical editorial solution | Editorial / Production as applicable | Do not prescribe editorial solution. |
| Performance interpretation, tactic, delivery, reaction | Character & Acting | Track evidence-based carry only. |
| Visual design, costume, prop, environment, palette, appearance design | Art Director | Track state/condition only. |
| Wording, naturalness, register, language polish | Shared Language & Voice QA | Exclude unless stated fact/state continuity is implicated. |
| Cross-scene state comparison | Continuity | Observe, compare, classify, flag, and route; no repair. |
| Conflict with no formally known authority | Record both sources and request authority clarification | Do not create precedence. |
