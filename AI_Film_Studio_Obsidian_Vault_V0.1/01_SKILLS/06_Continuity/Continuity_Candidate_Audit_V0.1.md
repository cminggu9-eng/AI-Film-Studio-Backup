## Selection and diversity judgment

The five CORE candidates deliberately combine an industry competency checklist, two accredited-training perspectives and two practitioner books. They are not five celebrity interviews. However, only CT-CA-01 has a sufficiently complete lawful body available among those five; this is a material readiness gap, not a reason to lower the access rule.

Potential future complementarity only: CT-CA-01 gives detailed physical/time/recording method; CT-CA-03 and CT-CA-05 indicate training/workflow coverage; CT-CA-09 and CT-CA-10 may supply independent book-length craft methods after lawful access; CT-CA-07 protects against “continuity = sameness.” No consensus, conflict finding or Cross-Distillation has occurred.
