# Continuity Decision Skeleton Draft V0.1

This is a capability-level decision skeleton supported by CT-D relationships and Draft Rules. It is not a Runtime flow, API, schema, data model, final state token set, or automatic repair process.

## Draft sequence

| Step | Draft judgment action | Supporting relationships / rules | Constraint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Identify the assigned, locatable evidence and relevant comparison domain. | RL-01; DR-01 | Do not turn an unshown claim into fact. |
| 2 | Establish the cited baseline and claimed/current state as separate named dimensions. | RL-05, RL-06, RL-12; DR-06, DR-07, DR-13 | Track only decision-relevant state; no required record form. |
| 3 | Compare the stated change against temporal frame, story order, causal relation, presence, and spatial relation where each is evidenced. | RL-02, RL-04, RL-09; DR-05, DR-08, DR-09 | Do not invent chronology, bridge events, or camera/staging solutions. |
| 4 | Separate audience exposure, character knowledge, belief, statement, and unshown information. | RL-03, RL-10; DR-03, DR-12 | Belief, suspicion, deception, and dramatic irony are not automatically Canon fact. |
| 5 | Check whether explicit time passage, off-screen interval, flashback, montage, nonlinearity, ambiguity, or other supported presentation condition changes the comparison meaning. | RL-08, RL-11, RL-13; DR-04, DR-10, DR-11 | No blanket exception and no Continuity authorization of deviation. |
| 6 | Produce only a provisional evidence outcome: supported continuity, unresolved/uncertain, or potential contradiction. | RL-03, RL-07, RL-12; DR-02, DR-12, DR-14 | These are descriptive draft outcomes, not Phase 4 canonical tokens or a severity scale. |
| 7 | Identify the lawful owner and issue a bounded evidence/context handoff when needed. | RL-07, RL-12; DR-02, DR-14 | Continuity does not repair, rewrite, stage, design, edit, decide Canon, or rank upstream authorities. |

## Draft state categories

The following are review categories only, not a storage schema: fact/reference; identity designation; audience/character knowledge; time/order; location/presence/spatial relation; object/appearance/physical condition; performance carry; and relevant revision context.

Relationship State and Environment / Set-State remain weak non-blocking depth gaps. Change Authorization remains weak; Severity and Retcon methods remain no-evidence gaps. CT-C30 remains DEFERRED.
