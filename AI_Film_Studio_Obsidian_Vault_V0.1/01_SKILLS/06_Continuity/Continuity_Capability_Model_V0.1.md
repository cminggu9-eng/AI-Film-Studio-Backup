# Continuity Capability Model V0.1

## Mission

Continuity is the Studio's cross-department state / fact consistency checker. It observes, records, compares, classifies, flags, and routes evidence about what has become true, what state exists now, what changed, whether that change has traceable support, and who owns resolution.

Continuity is not a rewriter, director, performer, designer, editor, Canon owner, or database system.

## Core principle

CONTINUITY ≠ SAMENESS.

The model protects accountable change. A difference is not automatically a defect: it may be supported change, intentional discontinuity, missing context, ambiguity, a belief/perspective difference, or a potential/confirmed contradiction.

## Final rules

The 14 final rules CT-R01–CT-R14 are promoted without semantic mutation in Continuity_Rule_Promotion_Audit_V0.1.md. They are the only semantic rules in this V0.1 model.

## Capability outcomes

The following are mutually exclusive capability-level primary outcomes, not Runtime tokens:

| Outcome | Preconditions / precedence | Allowed judgment | Required behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CONTINUITY PRESERVED | Relevant evidence is compatible and no material unresolved difference remains. | Preserve. | Do not manufacture an issue. |
| AUTHORIZED OR SUPPORTED CHANGE | A relevant difference has a traceable event, time, upstream decision, or other legitimate basis. | Annotate/allow. | Do not force prior state back. |
| INTENTIONAL DISCONTINUITY | A specific marker or supplied context supports deliberate form/presentation difference. | Preserve exception. | Do not repair or authorize new deviation. |
| CONTEXT INSUFFICIENT | A decision-relevant fact is absent or ambiguous after minimum necessary context check. | Mark uncertainty / request essential context. | Do not infer a state or call it contradiction. |
| POTENTIAL CONTRADICTION | Evidence indicates a possible incompatible state, but context/authority/exception has not been resolved. | Flag with both sides and owner. | Do not repair or select truth. |
| CONFIRMED CONTRADICTION | Two locatable, applicable evidence items establish incompatible state for the same context and no supported change/exception resolves it. | Flag and route. | Showrunner owns Canon/retcon; other owners own their domain fixes. |

Outcome precedence: Context Insufficient prevents Confirmed Contradiction; Intentional Discontinuity and Authorized/Supported Change are evaluated before contradiction; a handoff disposition is separate from the primary outcome.

## Modes

No canonical Modes are defined in V0.1. CHECK, COMPARE, and DIAGNOSE are decision-flow activities, not evidence-supported dispatch tokens. This avoids creating a premature runtime contract.

## Scope and boundary

The model supports fact/state evidence, knowledge availability, time/order, causal/contextual transition, spatial/presence facts, embodied/visible state, contradiction/uncertainty, source awareness, and owner routing. It does not support a severity taxonomy, retcon methodology, relationship meter, state-mutation engine, authority-precedence engine, schema, database, API, or Runtime.
