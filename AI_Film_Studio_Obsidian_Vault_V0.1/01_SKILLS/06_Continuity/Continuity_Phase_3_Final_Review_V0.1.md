## Core Continuity Architecture

| Area | Cross-distilled result |
| --- | --- |
| Fact / Canon | Locatable evidence, comparison, flag, and Showrunner handoff; no Canon creation. |
| Identity | Designation is distinct from presence and condition; no string-only identity rule. |
| Knowledge / information timing | Audience exposure, character knowledge, belief, suspicion, deception, and hidden material stay distinct. |
| Time / causality / transition | Compare declared time, story order, explicit cause-result, and lawful change without inventing chronology or bridge events. |
| Spatial / presence | Compare factual relations and modality only; no camera, blocking, coverage, or sound solution. |
| Props / appearance / physical / performance | Use named, evidenced dimensions; do not force sameness, diagnose, redesign, or direct performance. |
| Contradiction / uncertainty | Distinguish supported continuity, potential contradiction, and unresolved unknown; no severity scale. |
| Intentional discontinuity | Marker-specific protection for montage, flashback, ellipsis, nonlinearity, and ambiguity; no authorization of deviation. |
| State snapshot | Existing reference material supports traceable before/after comparison only; no schema or system. |
| Ownership routing | Evidence/context flag to lawful owner; Continuity remains checker, not owner. |
| Anti-overcorrection | Variation, time passage, legal off-screen transition, ambiguity, and formal presentation are not automatic errors. |
