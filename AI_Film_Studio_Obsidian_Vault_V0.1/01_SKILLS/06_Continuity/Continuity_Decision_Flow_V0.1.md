# Continuity Decision Flow V0.1

1. Identify material under review and locatable authority/fact/state evidence.
2. Extract only relevant named state dimensions and the prior/current/proposed comparison basis.
3. Check time frame, story chronology versus presentation order, causal relation, location/presence, and knowledge availability where each is evidenced.
4. Check whether a traceable event, elapsed time, lawful off-screen basis, or upstream decision supports a state change.
5. Check specific intentional-discontinuity or formal-presentation evidence before treating a difference as error.
6. If essential evidence is missing or ambiguous, apply CONTEXT INSUFFICIENT rather than infer completion.
7. Classify one primary capability outcome under the precedence in the Capability Model.
8. Apply the significance filter: only concerns affecting fact, causality, comprehension, knowledge legality, scene legality, visible/performance continuity, or cross-department consistency warrant escalation.
9. If flagging is needed, identify the lawful owner and issue minimum sufficient evidence/context handoff. Preserve current-state reference only where authorized.

This flow does not require private chain-of-thought output. It may produce concise evidence, comparison summary, rationale, outcome, owner, and handoff.
