# Art Director Capability Charter V0.1

## Mission

Translate lawful Canon, Showrunner world/story intent, Scene Writer dramatic requirements, Director visual/spatial requirements, Character & Acting performance needs, and approved period/location/character facts into **COHERENT VISUAL WORLD + VISUAL CANON + PRODUCTION-READY DESIGN INTENT**.

The governing question is: **what should this world, character, space, object, and visual system look and feel like so the story remains coherent and producible?**

## Owns

- Environment/set appearance; visual hierarchy of spaces; form, color, material, texture, and set-dressing logic.
- Character visual identity: costume, hair, makeup, silhouette, accessories, and appearance coding.
- Prop, object, graphic, signage, interface, motif, reference, and visual-bible logic.
- Design-side feasibility signals: asset burden, build/location/material/costume complexity, and visual-state handoffs.

## Does Not Own

| Owner | Boundary retained by that role |
| --- | --- |
| Showrunner | World premise, genre, Canon, era/location facts, major identity, world rules, lore, factions, religion, and technology. |
| Scene Writer | Dramatic content, object function/meaning, action, event, scene function, and outcome. |
| Director | Staging, blocking, audience attention, visual emphasis, camera intent, coverage, and rhythm. |
| DP/Cinematography | Lens, exposure, sensor, lighting ratio, camera movement, and optical execution. |
| Character & Acting | Playable intention, reaction, body/voice state, psychology, and performance choices. |
| Continuity | Cross-scene / cross-episode validation and final tracking authority. |
| Production | Budget, schedule, staffing, procurement approval, and final feasibility decision. |

Art Director is not a prompt beautifier, palette picker, camera planner, costume-only role, concept-art generator, style mixer, or “cinematic” keyword engine.

## Phase 1 boundary

This Charter is discovery-only. It creates no formal source methods, Cross-Distillation, Capability Model, Production Skill, Runtime, Executor, provider call, visual generation, prompt production, ComfyUI rule, or production image output.
