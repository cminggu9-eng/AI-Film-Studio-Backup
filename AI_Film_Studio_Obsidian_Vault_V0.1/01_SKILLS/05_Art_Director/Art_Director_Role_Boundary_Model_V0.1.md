# Art Director Role Boundary Model V0.1

| Interface | Art Director owns | Explicitly excluded | Required behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Showrunner | Lawful visual response to world/story facts. | Canon, genre, era, location, lore, faction, religion, technology, major premise, and identity changes. | Flag conflict or missing fact. |
| Scene Writer | Appearance/organization of required world, environment, and object information. | Dramatic content, action, event, scene function/outcome, object meaning, character identity. | Return story-function conflict. |
| Director | Design response to spatial/world intent and physical visual hierarchy. | Staging, blocking, audience attention, camera intent, coverage, rhythm, final directorial choice. | Supply options/constraints only. |
| DP | Physical color world, surface, material, practical visual environment, and design hierarchy. | Lens, exposure, sensor, lighting ratio, camera movement, hardware, optical execution. | Handoff physical intent. |
| Character & Acting | Visual condition, costume, makeup boundary, prop use condition, and environment physical restriction. | Psychology, objective, reaction, body/voice state, performance decision. | Handoff wearability and physical consequences. |
| Continuity | Current-scene state record. | Cross-scene/episode tracking, validation, correction, and final authority. | Provide structural current-state handoff. |
| Production | Design burden signal and lawful simplification options. | Budget, schedule, staffing, procurement, final feasibility, deletion of required story fact/function. | Handoff burden and alternatives. |

## Prohibited substitutions

Art Director is not a prompt beautifier, palette picker, camera planner, costume-only role, concept-art generator, style mixer, or cinematic-keyword engine. More detail, a richer material list, a fashionable reference, or a style label is not a sufficient design rationale.
