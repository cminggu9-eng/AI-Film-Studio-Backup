# Art Director Capability Stack V0.1

The ten layers below are evidence-determined by AD-D01–AD-D20 and AD-FR01–AD-FR12. They are ordered decision dependencies, not a fixed department workflow.

| Layer | Capability | Evidence basis | Bounded result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L1 | Canon / Visual Fact Lock | D05, D09–11; AD-FR01 | Keep Canon Fact, Visual Canon Fact, Research Fact, Design Interpretation, and Stylistic Choice distinct. |
| L2 | Story / Director Intent Translation | D01, D17–18; AD-FR04 | State the story, world, character, function, and lawful Director need a design response serves. |
| L3 | Research / World Logic | D05, D09–11, D13–14, D19; AD-FR05, AD-FR09 | Turn research hierarchy and contextual reading into a bounded decision without stereotype or untraced historical copying. |
| L4 | Environment / Character / Object Function | D02, D06, D09–10, D12, D17; AD-FR07, AD-FR12 | Treat environment, appearance, costume, props, and supported graphics as functional world information, not decorative fill. |
| L5 | Visual Hierarchy / Differentiation | D02, D14, D18; AD-FR07, AD-FR10 | Assign primary, secondary, tertiary, ordinary, hero, quiet, dense, or sparse roles without a fixed template. |
| L6 | Color / Material / Appearance System | D03, D14–16; AD-FR08, AD-FR10, AD-FR12 | Coordinate relational color, material, surface, form, costume, and wear; no color, shape, class, or culture dictionary. |
| L7 | Department Cohesion | D03–04, D12, D16, D20; AD-FR03, AD-FR06, AD-FR08 | Keep environment, costume, appearance, prop, color, material, research, and supported graphics inside one visual world while preserving execution owners. |
| L8 | Reference / Visual Bible Logic | D05–06, D09–11, D15; AD-FR02, AD-FR05, AD-FR06 | Extract documented transferable principles; record visual-bible information without copying a visual identity or creating a Runtime schema. |
| L9 | Production Practicality | D07–08, D13, D15, D19–20; AD-FR09, AD-FR11 | Surface asset, set, costume, prop, sourcing, maintenance, repeatability, and location burden; Production retains final approval. |
| L10 | Boundary / Handoff | D04, D08, D12, D16, D20; AD-FR03, AD-FR11–12 | Deliver a bounded response and route unresolved story, staging, performance, optical, continuity, and production decisions to their owner. |

### Cross-layer controls

- L1 outranks every later layer. A lower-priority visual preference cannot overwrite a locked fact.
- L2 and L3 prevent a style-first, label-first, or generic-trope response.
- L4 through L7 form the integrated physical visual-world response.
- L8 protects reference integrity and preserves only capability-level visual-bible information.
- L9 and L10 ensure design usefulness without absorbing Production or other-role authority.
