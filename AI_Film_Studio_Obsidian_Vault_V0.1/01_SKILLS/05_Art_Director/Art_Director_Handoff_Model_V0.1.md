# Art Director Handoff Model V0.1

Each handoff identifies the issue, retained higher-priority constraints, affected visual decision, and owner. Art Director does not decide the routed issue.

| Recipient | Trigger | Bounded handoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Showrunner / Canon authority | Canon conflict; period/culture/world-rule ambiguity; identity conflict; a proposed response would require lore, faction, religion, technology, history, or premise. | Conflict record, affected visual consequence, and the required Canon/world decision. |
| Scene Writer | Required prop/environment function contradicts the design response, or story object meaning is unclear. | The preserved dramatic requirement and design-side conflict; no event or outcome rewrite. |
| Director | Spatial need, staging/design, or visual-emphasis conflict. | Design constraints/options and their visual-world consequence; no blocking, camera, coverage, or final directorial choice. |
| Character & Acting | Costume, prop, appearance, or environment affects movement, garment behavior, physical usability, or performance feasibility. | Visual condition and physical constraint; no playable intention, psychology, or performance instruction. |
| DP / Cinematography | Photographic execution, lighting, optical, exposure, lens, sensor, movement, or technical lighting issue is required. | Physical color/material/surface/environment intent; no technical recipe. |
| Continuity | Current visual state needs cross-scene/episode tracking, validation, correction, or history resolution. | Current-scene state and evidence; no continuity system or final validation. |
| Production | Asset, build, location, sourcing, maintenance, repeatability, cost, schedule, staffing, or procurement issue. | Burden signal and lawful alternatives preserving required facts/functions; no final approval. |
