# Art Director Production Skill Outcome Audit V0.1

Phase 4 formally defines six mutually exclusive capability outcomes and an ordered precedence rule. The staging Skill preserves each exact token and the rule to select the highest applicable outcome while recording other issues as handoffs.

| Precedence | Outcome | Exact Phase 4 meaning preserved | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CONTEXT_RESOLUTION_REQUIRED | A material Canon, place/period/culture, identity, prop-function, or Director-versus-Canon conflict prevents a lawful visual decision. | PASS |
| 2 | CROSS_ROLE_DECISION_REQUIRED | The requested decision belongs to Showrunner, Scene Writer, Director, DP, Character & Acting, Continuity, or Production. | PASS |
| 3 | RESEARCH_DECISION_REQUIRED | A research-sensitive claim needed for the design response remains unresolved. | PASS |
| 4 | PRODUCTION_FEASIBILITY_DECISION_REQUIRED | The design response is identified, but cost, schedule, sourcing, build, or approval needs Production's decision. | PASS |
| 5 | PARTIAL_OR_DEFERRED_CAPABILITY_LIMIT | The request reaches an explicitly partial, no-evidence, or deferred capability; only the documented bounded response is available. | PASS |
| 6 | DESIGN_RESPONSE_READY | A lawful, sufficiently traced Art Director response and any non-blocking handoffs are ready. | PASS |
