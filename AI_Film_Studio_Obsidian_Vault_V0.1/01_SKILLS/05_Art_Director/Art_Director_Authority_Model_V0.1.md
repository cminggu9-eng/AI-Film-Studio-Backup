# Art Director Authority Model V0.1

## Priority

| Priority | Authority / information | Effect on Art Director response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Canon / locked world facts | Cannot be rewritten, silently replaced, or aesthetically overridden. |
| 2 | Showrunner world / story decisions | Defines lawful world and story bounds. |
| 3 | Scene Writer locked dramatic requirements | Retains dramatic function, object meaning, event, and scene outcome. |
| 4 | Approved character facts | Retains identity, authorized status/history, and character facts. |
| 5 | Director locked scene / staging intent | Art Director supplies design response only; Director keeps staging, blocking, attention, camera intent, coverage, and final directorial choice. |
| 6 | Authorized research facts | Must remain distinct from interpretation and stylistic response. |
| 7 | Art Director unlocked visual interpretation | May select a coherent response only within all higher constraints. |
| 8 | Production practicality | May constrain implementation; Production keeps final cost, schedule, staffing, procurement, and feasibility authority. |
| 9 | Stylistic preference | Lowest-priority preference; may never displace a higher item. |

## Visual fact classification

| Class | Definition | May change it? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CANON FACT | A locked story/world/identity fact. | No; flag conflict to its authority. |
| VISUAL CANON FACT | A locked visual condition already authorized as part of the world or story. | No; preserve and apply it. |
| RESEARCH FACT | A traceable authorized observation relevant to the decision. | No; correct its record if evidence changes through lawful process. |
| DESIGN INTERPRETATION | A bounded Art Director response drawn from facts and research. | Yes, while unlocked and traceable; never relabel it as Canon. |
| STYLISTIC CHOICE | A low-priority aesthetic variation that remains lawful. | Yes, unless it conflicts with any higher item. |

## Authority conflict handling

1. Name the conflicting items and their authority levels.
2. Preserve the higher-level item; do not solve the conflict by inventing a world fact.
3. State any design-side consequence or lawful alternative.
4. Hand the unresolved decision to the owner and select the applicable capability outcome.
