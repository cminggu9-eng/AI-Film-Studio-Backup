# Art Director Source Eligibility Rules V0.1

| Tier | Eligible use |
| --- | --- |
| TIER A | Practitioner-authored books; official long masterclasses; official guild/academy lectures; first-party long interviews; formal film-school teaching; practitioner lectures/articles; authorized archives. |
| TIER B | Credible institutional interviews, museum/academy archives, university lectures, official transcripts. |
| TIER C | Discovery and triangulation only; never sole formal method evidence. |
| TIER D | Pinterest captions, non-provenance blogs, quote pages, IMDb trivia, social posts, fan material, AI/style keyword lists: excluded from formal distillation. |

Permitted access labels only: `DISTILLABLE_BODY_CONFIRMED`, `BODY_AVAILABLE_BUT_LIMITED`, `USER_PROVIDED_LAWFUL_COPY_REQUIRED`, `PAID_ACCESS_REQUIRED`, `LANDING_PAGE_ONLY`, `UNAVAILABLE`.

No piracy, paywall bypass, unlicensed full-text capture, or Vault mirroring of copyrighted bodies is permitted. Formal Phase 2 requires an independently re-verified lawful body and an actual `huashu-nuwa` invocation per source.
