# Art Director Role Boundary Audit V0.1

| Interface | Art Director may do | Must not do | Required handoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Showrunner | Visualize lawful world facts and story intent | Change era, place, identity, lore, genre, faction, religion, technology, or Canon | Flag contradiction / missing visual fact to Showrunner |
| Scene Writer | Design the appearance and organization of required objects/actions | Add dramatic function or alter object meaning/outcome | Flag design conflict without rewriting scene |
| Director | Supply spatial/world options and design contrast | Decide blocking, shot, camera, coverage, or rhythm | Design constraints/options to Director |
| DP/Cinematography | State materials, practical-source intent, color relationships, surface response, and environmental hierarchy | Specify lens, sensor, exposure, lighting ratio, camera movement, or optical execution | Design intent / material swatches to DP |
| Character & Acting | Design outward appearance and feasible costume/makeup conditions | Create psychology, playable intention, reaction, or body/voice state | Wearability/appearance constraints to C&A |
| Continuity | Emit current-scene visual state: dirt, damage, blood, wear, props, signage, dressing, transformations | Validate cross-scene or episode history | Visual-state record to Continuity |
| Production | Flag asset/build/location/material/crowd burden and lawful simplification options | Approve budget, schedule, crew, procurement, or delete story function | Burden/options to Production |

Boundary verdict: `PASS`. No authority expansion is authorized in Phase 1.
