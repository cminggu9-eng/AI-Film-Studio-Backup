## Architecture

- Capability Stack: ten evidence-determined layers, from Visual Fact Lock through bounded handoff.
- Decision Flow: fact lock → story/world/Director intent → research posture → world/function → hierarchy → color/material/form → cohesion → reference/copy risk → burden → handoff.
- Authority Priority: Canon/world facts, Showrunner, Scene Writer, character facts, Director intent, research, Art Director interpretation, Production practicality, stylistic preference.
- Context Sufficiency: only material unresolved visual facts/conflicts block; missing moodboard, style name, palette, AI prompt, or ComfyUI workflow does not.
