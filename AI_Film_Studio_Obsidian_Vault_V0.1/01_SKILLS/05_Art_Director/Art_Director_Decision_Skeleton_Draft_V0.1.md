# Art Director Decision Skeleton Draft V0.1

1. Confirm lawful Canon, period/place/identity facts, story requirements, and authorized current visual state.
2. Receive Scene Writer dramatic requirements and Director spatial/visual intent without taking their decisions.
3. Establish research hierarchy and mark observed fact, interpreted reference, and permitted stylization.
4. State the visual-world problem and the story/world/function a design response must serve.
5. Define the selected concept and visual hierarchy; choose what is primary, supporting, ordinary, sparse, or dense.
6. Develop environment, material, color, form, costume, props and supported graphics as one physical world.
7. Translate intent into task-appropriate references, layout, samples, artifacts and briefs; never copy a reference identity.
8. Coordinate with Director, DP, Character & Acting and Production through bounded design-side handoffs.
9. Identify controllability, sourcing, build, repetition and asset burden; offer lawful alternatives when required.
10. Emit current visual-state and constraint handoff; Continuity/Production retain cross-scene/final authority.

Trace: AD-DR01–12. This is an architecture draft, not Runtime schema, prompt format, or final Capability Model.
