# Art Director Production Skill Mode Audit V0.1

Phase 4 formally defines three canonical Modes. The staging Skill preserves their exact names, count, purpose, authority, allowed operations, no-change behavior, and handoff behavior.

| Mode | Exact Phase 4 semantic preserved | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DESIGN | Form a new lawful visual-world response inside Art Director authority; lock facts, make a research decision, set hierarchy, offer alternatives, and emit minimum-sufficient outputs. Do not invent material facts or force an aesthetic answer through essential conflict; hand off factual, dramatic, staging, performance, optical, continuity, and production decisions. | PASS |
| REVISE | Reassess only an unlocked Design Interpretation after lawful changed input or valid design concern; retain/narrow/replace/simplify and update trace/current-state handoff. Preserve an existing response when no lawful change exists; never revise Canon or another role's decision; return ownership conflicts. | PASS |
| DIAGNOSE | Identify cause/scope of visual logic, reference, generic-trope, authority, or burden issue; classify it and offer bounded design-side options only. Report no Art Director change when outside role/unsupported; send decision request and retained constraints to owner. | PASS |
