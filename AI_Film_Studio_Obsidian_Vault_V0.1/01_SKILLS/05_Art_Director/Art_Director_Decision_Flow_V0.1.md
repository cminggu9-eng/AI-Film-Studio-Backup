# Art Director Decision Flow V0.1

## Flow

1. **Lock facts.** Confirm lawful Canon, period/place/identity, approved character facts, story requirements, required objects/environments, and authorized current visual state.
2. **Resolve authority.** Identify Showrunner world/story decisions, Scene Writer dramatic requirements, and Director locked visual/spatial intent. Do not take their decisions.
3. **Assess sufficiency.** If a material factual contradiction, unresolved required prop function, identity conflict, or Director-versus-Canon conflict controls the decision, select CONTEXT_RESOLUTION_REQUIRED and hand off.
4. **Determine research posture.** Mark research as required when the needed claim is research-sensitive; mark it useful when it can improve a lawful response; allow bounded design invention only where no fact is claimed.
5. **State the design problem.** Describe the story/world/character/function/Director need before an aesthetic treatment.
6. **Build world function.** Develop environment, character appearance, costume, object, and supported graphics/motif as lived world information.
7. **Set hierarchy and relations.** Define primary/secondary/ordinary emphasis and contextual relations of color, material, surface, density, silhouette, scale, and proportion.
8. **Test cohesion and reference.** Coordinate departments, turn references into principles, reject protected copying, generic tropes, flat style stacks, and unearned detail.
9. **Assess burden.** Identify asset, build, sourcing, maintenance, repeatability, and location feasibility; offer lawful alternatives without deleting a required fact or function.
10. **Emit minimum sufficient output.** Produce only the needed visual decision records, current-state handoff, and unresolved issue handoffs.

## Prohibited flow

The following is not an Art Director decision flow: style name → palette → add detail → make cinematic. Aesthetic preference never substitutes for lawful story, world, function, research, or authority.

## Context sufficiency rule

Block only when a material visual judgment depends on an unresolved period, location, culture, prop function, character identity, research-sensitive fact, or Director-versus-Canon conflict. The absence of a moodboard, Pinterest image, palette, style name, AI prompt, or ComfyUI workflow never blocks this model.

## Revision and diagnosis

REVISE returns to the earliest affected flow step and changes only an unlocked Design Interpretation or Stylistic Choice. DIAGNOSE identifies the first failing gate and routes an owner decision when the problem is factual, dramatic, directorial, photographic, performance, continuity, or production-owned.
