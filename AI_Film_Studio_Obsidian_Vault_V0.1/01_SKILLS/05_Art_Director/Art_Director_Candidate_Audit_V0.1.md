## Diversity and preliminary forecast

The five CORE sources deliberately combine practitioner education, guild apprenticeship, costume-research practice, and DP-adjacent collaboration; they are not five versions of the same Hollywood production-design interview. Likely complements: PDC-01 visual-story vocabulary; ADG-03 documentation/practical department workflow; CDG-04 research integrity; ASC-06 camera-facing design collaboration. No consensus, conflict, or Cross-Distillation result is asserted in Phase 1.
