# Character & Acting Capability Charter V0.1

## Mission

Translate lawful Canon, character facts and knowledge state, Showrunner character intent, Scene Writer dramatic situation/objective/resistance/turn, and any supplied Director staging intent into **playable performance interpretation**.

The question is: **How does this character live, react, and perform this dramatic moment?**

## Owns

- Character intention, playable action/tactic, listening, reaction, subtext implication, emotional transition, and observable behavior.
- Character-specific performance choices, performance scale/restraint, speech as performance action, and clear scene-local performance-state handoff.
- Performance needs, physical feasibility, reaction needs, and staging-performance conflicts for lawful Director handoff.

## Does Not Own

| Owner | Boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Showrunner | Character premise, arc, relationship facts, locked story intent, episode/series transformation. |
| Scene Writer | Dialogue wording, dramatic construction, required action, objective, resistance, turn, and outcome. Unplayable material is flagged, not rewritten. |
| Director | Staging, blocking authority, spatial intent, audience attention, camera/coverage, and directorial rhythm. |
| Shared Language & Voice QA | Text fluency, naturalness, register, wording defects, and language integrity. |
| Art Director | Character visual design, costume, makeup, hairstyle, visual identity, and production design. |
| Continuity | Cross-scene and cross-episode validation of knowledge, injury, fatigue, relationship, and emotional carry. |

Character & Acting is not an emotion-label system, a micro-expression prompt library, a dialogue-polishing layer, an action filler, a camera plan, or a story-rewrite authority.

## Phase 1 Boundary

This Charter is discovery-only. It creates no formal methods, Capability Model, Production Skill, Runtime, Executor, provider call, real-performance output, animation rule, or AI-specific constraint.
