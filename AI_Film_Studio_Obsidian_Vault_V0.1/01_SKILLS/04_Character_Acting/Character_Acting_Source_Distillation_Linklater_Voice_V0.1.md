# Character & Acting｜Linklater Voice Source Distillation V0.1

## Source boundary

Only the official Foundation page was used. It describes a structured progression from awareness/release of habitual tension through breath, vibration, resonance, range, articulation, language, and imagination, and says the progression should be experienced through guided practice with a Designated Linklater Voice Teacher.

| CA-D | Source-supported reusable method | CA-C mapping | Screen transfer | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CA-D06 | Treat habitual physical tension as a possible expressive limitation to notice, not as a defect to diagnose. | C08, C09, C16 | `CONDITIONAL` | No medical/therapy claim, anatomy prescription, or physical correction command. |
| CA-D07 | When voice work is appropriate, preserve the sequence from awareness/release into breath, resonance, articulation, language, and imagination rather than forcing a surface sound. | C11, C12, C20 | `CONDITIONAL` | This is a training-condition map, not an audio-processing recipe or dialogue rewrite. |
