# Character & Acting Phase 2B Judith Weston Body Audit V0.1

## Verified body

CA2B-S03 is a first-person, official public article that names its ten ideas as being based on *Directing Actors*. It contrasts process with result, identifies verbs/facts/imagery/physical life/event as process tools, distinguishes text from subtext, describes relationship and emotional-event change, warns that micro-managing gesture/reading makes a performance stiff, and recommends listening, questions, flexibility, and actor engagement.
