# Character & Acting Phase 2B Meisner Body Audit V0.1

## Verified bodies

CA2B-S01 describes the official exercise system—repetition, improvisation, independent activity, and scene work—and identifies its developmental result: concentration, emotional availability, truthful response within imaginary circumstances, and an organic/spontaneous/disciplined instrument. CA2B-S02 supplies additional explanatory body: stepwise exercise progression; feelings not pushed/faked; imagined circumstances plus real doing; partner attention; response to the other person in unanticipated moments; individual adaptation.

## Sufficiency decision

`DISTILLABLE_BODY_CONFIRMED` for a **narrow transferable capability**: attention, reception, unplanned response, preparation/live-response separation, and individual adjustment. It is not sufficient to recreate a complete Meisner curriculum, literal exercises, or universal acting doctrine.

## Rejected candidate outputs

- A runtime requirement to perform repetition, independent activity, or improvisation drills.
- “Do not think,” “all acting must improvise,” or any school-supremacy rule.
- Emotion labels, forced feeling, facial/micro-expression instructions, or stage-only claim.
