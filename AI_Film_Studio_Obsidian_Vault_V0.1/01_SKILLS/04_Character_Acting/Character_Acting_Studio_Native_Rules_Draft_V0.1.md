# Character & Acting Studio-Native Rules Draft V0.1

| Rule | Class | Origin / relationship | Draft rule | Conditions and boundary / transfer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CA-DR01 | `HARD` | D01/D15/D18; RL11 | Preserve supplied character facts, knowledge, text, and locked dramatic facts; performance interpretation cannot add or rewrite them. | Handoff impossible material; direct text/fact boundary. |
| CA-DR02 | `HARD` | D03/D11/D17; RL05 | Do not convert emotion or intent into deterministic facial/micro-expression prescriptions. | C10 remains prohibited; emotion can still be contextual information. |
| CA-DR03 | `HARD` | D09/D14/D17; RL10 | Do not take Director staging/blocking/camera/coverage/final-choice authority; flag and hand off conflicts. | Collaboration trace only; transfer depends on source method. |
| CA-DR04 | `DEFAULT` | D03/D14; RL01 | Prefer a playable action/process over a bare requested result. | Result language may describe context but is insufficient as the only actionable guidance. |
| CA-DR05 | `DEFAULT` | D05/D10/D11/D16; RL02/RL04 | Before selecting a response, account for what the partner or situation has actually made available. | Receive/change/respond may be internal, delayed, concealed, still, or refusal; no visible action required per line. |
| CA-DR06 | `DEFAULT` | D13/D17/D18; RL08/RL12 | Give bounded, character-specific playable questions or adjustments while preserving discovery. | No generic behavior library, diagnosis, or free alteration of Canon/outcome. |
| CA-DR07 | `CONDITIONAL` | D04/D12; RL07 | Use private preparation only when it supports present action without predetermining response or becoming character fact. | Never expose private As-If as Canon. |
| CA-DR08 | `CONDITIONAL` | D06–D09/D03/D14; RL03/RL06/RL10 | Body, voice, timing, and physical behavior may serve current intention/relationship/response when supported; otherwise do not decorate. | Conditional transfers; no therapy, voice recipe, Shared QA rewrite, or specialist imitation. |
| CA-DR09 | `CONDITIONAL` | D05/D11/D17; RL05/RL09 | Stillness, silence, delayed response, concealment, and restraint can be playable when intention/listening/resistance supports them. | Do not enforce a visible beat, gesture, sigh, or expression. |
| CA-DR10 | `CONDITIONAL` | D15/D16/D18; RL09/RL11 | Keep text distinct from subtext and performance intention; mark potential relation to Scene Writer turn without merging them. | No dialogue rewrite or Scene Writer outcome change. |

Counts: Hard `3`; Default `3`; Conditional `4`; Optional `0`. Every Draft Rule has retained CA-D and relationship trace; unsupported Draft Rules: `0`.
