# Character & Acting Source Eligibility Rules V0.1

## Eligibility Tiers

| Tier | Eligible material | Phase 1 use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TIER A | Formal books by teacher/author, official course bodies, official curriculum, first-party long-form lectures, official masterclasses, guild/academy/institute resources | Preferred formal source body when lawfully accessible. |
| TIER B | Credible institutional interview, full transcript, archival lecture, official educational video | Eligible supporting source body. |
| TIER C | Respected secondary explanation | Candidate discovery only; never a sole formal-method basis. |
| TIER D | Quote collections, TikTok/short tips, fan summary, blog snippet, AI-generated summary | Excluded from formal source distillation. |

## Source-Body Rule

Every candidate source must be classified as one of:

- `AVAILABLE NOW`
- `AVAILABLE WITH USER-PROVIDED LAWFUL COPY`
- `PAID ACCESS REQUIRED`
- `LANDING PAGE ONLY`
- `UNAVAILABLE`

Existence of a book, course, or celebrity association is not evidence that a distillable body is currently available.

## Legal and Scope Rules

- Do not download pirated books, use unauthorized full-text mirrors, bypass a paywall, use bootleg courses, or treat an illicit PDF as a source body.
- A paid or user-supplied lawful text remains `CONDITIONAL SOURCE` until legal access exists.
- Candidate evidence may establish possible contribution only. It may not be converted into CA-D methods in Phase 1.
- Screen/stage transfer limitations, school dogmatism, role drift, and source accessibility must remain explicit.
