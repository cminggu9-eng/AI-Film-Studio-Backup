# Character & Acting Decision Flow V0.1

1. Lock Canon, character facts, character knowledge, scene text and locked scene facts. If conflicting or missing in a performance-critical way, return `CONTEXT_REQUIRED` or `UPSTREAM_DECISION_REQUIRED`.
2. Separate Canon fact, scene fact, character knowledge, belief, and assumption. Audience knowledge never becomes character knowledge by default.
3. Establish given circumstances, immediate partner/relationship condition, objective, and playable intention—not an emotion-first label.
4. Check whether requested direction is only a result (“more sad”, “more angry”, “more feeling”). Preserve it as state/context if useful, then seek a playable process rather than a fixed emotion→verb conversion.
5. Receive available partner speech/action, environmental stimulus, relationship shift, or resistance before choosing a response.
6. Process/change/respond through action, tactic, delay, concealment, stillness, silence, refusal, withdrawal, or escalation as circumstances support; no visible behavior is mandatory per line.
7. Consider internal-to-observable channels conditionally: body, voice, pause, timing, attention, proximity, object relation, gesture, or stillness. Reject deterministic face/micro-expression recipes and decorative behavior.
8. Check character specificity, restraint, and performer freedom. A bounded playable constraint may guide discovery; it must not prescribe each blink, gaze, breath, or gesture.
9. Check role authority and emit required handoffs. Output current-scene performance state only as a structural Continuity handoff, not a continuity system.

No branch is `emotion label → facial expression → gesture`.
