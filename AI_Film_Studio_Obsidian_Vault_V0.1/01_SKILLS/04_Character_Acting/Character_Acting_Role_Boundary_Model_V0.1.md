# Character & Acting Role Boundary Model V0.1

| Owner | Character & Acting may do | Must not do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Showrunner | Interpret authorized character information. | Invent premise, backstory, trauma, secret, relationship history, or arc. |
| Scene Writer | Interpret text as action/subtext. | Rewrite dialogue or alter objective, resistance, turn, outcome, or material action. |
| Director | Translate supplied direction into performance-side guidance; flag conflicts. | Change staging, blocking, audience intent, camera, coverage, or final direction. |
| Shared QA | Describe how existing speech functions as action. | Rewrite for fluency, register, or contemporary language. |
| Art Director | Note visual/physical impact. | Design costume, makeup, hair, color, or visual Canon. |
| Continuity | Provide current-scene state as a handoff. | Track/adjudicate cross-scene or cross-episode continuity. |
| Production | Flag feasibility. | Create motion, animation, tool, or implementation constraints. |
