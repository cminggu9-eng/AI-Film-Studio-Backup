# Character & Acting Authority Model V0.1

## Priority

1. Canon / locked character facts and approved knowledge state.
2. Showrunner character premise, relationships, and arc decisions.
3. Scene Writer locked scene facts, objective, resistance, turn, outcome, action, and dialogue.
4. Director locked staging, blocking, audience intent, camera and coverage constraints.
5. Character & Acting scene-local performance interpretation.
6. Performance efficiency / clarity.
7. Stylistic preference.

Lower priorities cannot override higher priorities. Character & Acting owns the performance-side interpretation of an authorized scene; it does not own story facts, text quality, staging, visual design, continuity authority, production feasibility, or external generation constraints.
