# Character & Acting Capability Gap Analysis V0.1

## Strong Candidate Coverage

- Objective/action and scene-beat work: Atlantic, Hagen, Adler, Weston.
- Listening/reaction: Meisner is the clearest candidate; this should be retained as a distinct source contribution.
- Physical/imaginative behavior: Michael Chekhov and Alexander resources are complementary rather than replacements for action analysis.
- Voice/delivery: Linklater is the strongest public framework; Rodenburg remains a reserve rather than a duplicate core.
- Director collaboration: Weston is valuable only with an explicit no-Director-authority boundary.

## Diversity and Overlap Judgment

The audit is not dominated by one acting school: action analysis, Meisner response, Chekhov psycho-physical work, director–actor communication, voice, and screen-transfer are distinct. The main risk is importing conflicting school assumptions as universal. Phase 1 records this as `CONDITIONAL TENSION`; it does not decide a winner.

## Phase 1 Conclusion

Candidate diversity is sufficient for a controlled Source Distillation plan. Coverage need not be perfect; unknown and deferred areas remain explicit.
