# Character & Acting Handoff Model V0.1

| Trigger | Handoff target | Character & Acting action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Canon/character fact/knowledge ambiguity; new backstory or arc is needed | Showrunner / Canon owner | Flag, preserve known boundary, request decision. |
| Objective, resistance, turn, outcome, dialogue, or required action is contradictory/unplayable | Scene Writer | Flag the performance consequence; do not rewrite. |
| Staging, blocking, visibility, camera, coverage, or final directorial choice conflicts with performance need | Director | State performance-side requirement and conflict; do not alter staging. |
| Language defect prevents intended performance | Shared Language & Voice QA | Describe performance impact; do not polish text. |
| Costume, visual, prop, or environment condition blocks physical performance | Art Director | Describe required performance condition; do not redesign. |
| Current scene state needs later tracking | Continuity | Provide structural current-state handoff only; do not adjudicate cross-scene continuity. |
| Technical/physical feasibility requires review | Production | Flag feasibility condition; do not choose implementation. |
