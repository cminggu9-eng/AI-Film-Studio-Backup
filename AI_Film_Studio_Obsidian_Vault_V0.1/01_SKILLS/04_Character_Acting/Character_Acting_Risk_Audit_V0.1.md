# Character & Acting Risk Audit V0.1

| ID | Risk | Phase 1 control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RISK-01 | Emotion-label generator | Require circumstance, intention, action, resistance, response, and relationship; no label-to-preset rule. |
| RISK-02 | Mechanical micro-expression library | Preserve CA-C10 as a boundary; no facial charts or pseudo-science. |
| RISK-03 | Action on every dialogue line | Treat behavior as purposeful, not decorative filler. |
| RISK-04 | Generic AI acting clichés | Audit character-specific behavior and anti-cliché evidence. |
| RISK-05 | Unplayable psychology | Prefer playable action, response, timing, and physical condition. |
| RISK-06 | Scene Writer drift | Flag unplayable material; do not rewrite dialogue, event, or outcome. |
| RISK-07 | Director drift | Surface performance needs; do not claim blocking, camera, or coverage authority. |
| RISK-08 | Acting-school dogmatism | Preserve consensus, complementarity, tension, and conflict as predictions only. |
| RISK-09 | Missing listening/reaction | Treat receive-process-change-respond as a core discovery axis. |
| RISK-10 | One behavior template for all characters | Require character-specificity evidence. |
| RISK-11 | Overdirection | Audit bounded freedom; reject sub-second command schemas. |
| RISK-12 | Knowledge-state violation | Preserve Canon and character knowledge state. |
| RISK-13 | Locked character/arc violation | Retain Showrunner authority. |
| RISK-14 | Stage-to-screen mechanical transfer | Audit projection, scale, medium, and camera-context limits. |
| RISK-15 | Premature AI/animation constraints | Keep CA-C21 as future interface boundary only. |
