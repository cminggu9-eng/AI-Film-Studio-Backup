## Acceptance criteria for a future hardening run

1. Actual invocation evidence, not a statement that the Skill was read.
2. One source group per invocation; no unlogged aggregation.
3. Lawful exact source body and locators recorded before extraction.
4. Source-supported method, inference, and Studio synthesis visibly separated.
5. Frozen production artifacts remain untouched unless a separately authorized successor passes comparison and publish gates.

## Non-actions

No historical asset is redistilled in this task. No runtime, executor, provider, smoke test, Capability Model, Skill, archive, or controlled publish is started.
