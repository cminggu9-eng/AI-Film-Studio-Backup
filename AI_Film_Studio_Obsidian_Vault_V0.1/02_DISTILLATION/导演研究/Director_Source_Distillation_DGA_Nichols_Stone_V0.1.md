## B. Scope

This source documents a Director–DP–Production Designer collaboration around scene point of view, geography, visual purpose, movement, and location reality. It is used only for Director-facing decision methods and handoff logic. It supplies neither a Nichols/Stone visual style nor a production-design, lighting, or camera-technical operating system.

## D. Source-native methods

| Method ID | Reusable Director decision / work method | DR-C mapping | Transferability | Evidence trace |
