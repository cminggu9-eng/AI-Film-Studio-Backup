## B. Scope

This source supplies Director-facing methods for scene analysis, visual expression, actor-facing scene work, planned action execution, and constrained resource allocation. It does not create a Glatter style, an acting system, a storyboard mandate, a production-management system, or an editorial workflow.

## C. Verified evidence areas

| Area | High-fidelity paraphrase | Evidence trace |
|---|---|---|
| Scene analysis first | Glatter identifies text, subtext, scene turns, and scene category before deciding how to shoot it. | Q&A on storyteller approach, paragraph 135 |
| Visual scale and resources | She distinguishes major emotional scenes from transitions so limited resources can be allocated with a sense of visual scale. | paragraph 135 |
| Actor-facing turn work | She breaks down an interrogation exchange with actors around the moments where the relationship turns, then evaluates whether those turns occurred in a take. | paragraphs 155–162, 172–174 |
| Action preparation | For constrained action, she makes boards and a must-have / wish list ahead of shooting so the crew can execute rather than debate on the clock. | paragraphs 166–171 |
