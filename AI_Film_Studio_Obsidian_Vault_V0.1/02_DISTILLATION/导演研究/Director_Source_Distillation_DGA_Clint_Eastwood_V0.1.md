## B. Scope

This source supports Director-facing methods for spatial coherence, performance-attentive coverage, collaborative on-set adaptation, and story-first visual flow. It does not authorize one-take mythology, Eastwood-style imitation, default minimal coverage, department micro-management, or editorial/post workflow.

## C. Verified evidence areas

| Area | High-fidelity paraphrase | Evidence trace |
|---|---|---|
| Spatial coherence | Eastwood values a sustained feeling of room and scene geography and sees intermediate connective material as essential to constructing a scene. | “Eastwood on Constructing a Film,” paragraphs 175–179 |
| Performance-attentive staging | He uses an example where staying with an actor prevents unnecessary cutaways from weakening the performance moment. | paragraphs 175–177 |
| Responsive collaboration | He comes prepared but lets on-set discoveries and useful actor / Art / wardrobe contributions alter a plan when they improve the result. | paragraphs 147, 151–156 |
| Story over conspicuous display | He seeks visual flow whose rhythm supports story rather than camera movement that calls attention to the apparatus. | paragraphs 156–159 |
