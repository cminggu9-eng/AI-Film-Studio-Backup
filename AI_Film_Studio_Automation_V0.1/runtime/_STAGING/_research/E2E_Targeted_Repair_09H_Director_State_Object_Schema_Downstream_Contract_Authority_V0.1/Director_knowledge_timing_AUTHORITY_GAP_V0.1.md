# Director knowledge_timing Authority Gap V0.1

## Lawful purpose

The field preserves upstream-authorized knowledge/reveal timing for audience and characters. The Director must not disclose knowledge early, invent holders, or change reveal timing.

## Missing authority

The fixture compiler carries `knowledge_events`; Scene Writer records `knowledge_holders` and `reveal_status`; other historical examples use different names. No authority maps any of those to exact Director `knowledge_timing` keys, requires an audience/character taxonomy, or provides a value schema at this boundary.

## Decision required

Choose and document the lawful source-preserving projection of compiled `knowledge_events` into this field, including exact holder/reveal/unknown representation. This must not be inferred from historical model field names.
