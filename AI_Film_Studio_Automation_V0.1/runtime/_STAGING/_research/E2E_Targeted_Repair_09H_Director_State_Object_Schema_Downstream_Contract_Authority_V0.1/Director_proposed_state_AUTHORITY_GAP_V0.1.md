# Director proposed_state Authority Gap V0.1

## Lawful purpose

The field can record an allowed directorial state impact, but it is explicitly not Canon committed state and cannot decide a Showrunner-owned outcome, Scene Function, or story event.

## Missing authority

Fixtures define `authorized_transitions`; they do not define which Director-object keys express those transitions, whether this object may include other dimensions, or how a proposed directorial impact is distinguished from an upstream committed change. R13/R14 variants are observed only.

## Decision required

Define a stable proposed-impact model and its acceptance path: approved transition reference, immutable source, authority source, and whether the field is a projection of the compiled transition registry. Until then: `CONTRACT DESIGN REQUIRED`.
