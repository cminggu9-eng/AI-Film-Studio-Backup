# Art Director Minimum-Sufficient Validation Contract V0.1

Date: 2026-08-31  
Contract version: `ART-DIRECTOR-INTEGRATION-V0.2`

## Validation stages

1. Provider / parse validation: response existence, parseability, and durable raw evidence.
2. Integration machine-contract validation: exact fields, types, lawful absence, and closed structure.
3. Canonical-token validation: exact, case-sensitive frozen Art Director outcomes.
4. Authority-boundary validation: reject camera, performance, Canon, or Production approval takeover.

## Validator may require

- Exact required machine structure and field types.
- Canonical outcome tokens.
- Read-only Canon locks and prohibited-change carriage.
- Explicitly required content only.
- Lawful absence representations.
- Handoff or unresolved data only when its contract requires it.

## Validator may not require

- Fixed display headings or fixed English sections.
- Semantic richness, category fullness, or prose length beyond non-empty content.
- Production, feasibility, budget, or constraint keywords.
- A non-empty unresolved issue when none exists.
- Semantic auto-fill, cross-field repair, or post-hoc extraction.

Principle: `ABSENCE OF IRRELEVANT CONTENT IS NOT A FAILURE`.
