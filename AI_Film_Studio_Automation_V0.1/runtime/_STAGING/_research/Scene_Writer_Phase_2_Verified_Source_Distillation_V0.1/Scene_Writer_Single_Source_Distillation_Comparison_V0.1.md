# Scene Writer Single-Source Distillation Comparison V0.1

> This is a comparison of finished independent source outputs. It is **not** Cross-Distillation, a Capability Model, a Studio-Native Rule set, a router, or a Production Skill.

## Consensus Candidates (for a future authorized phase only)

|Candidate observation|BBC|Academy|WGF|Boundary|
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
|A scene needs an audience-available carrier for the event/information it claims.|Direct|Direct|Partial|Does not prescribe one visual grammar or ban approved narration.|
|Action and dialogue should be selected for what the audience can learn and what the interaction is doing.|Partial|Direct|Direct|Does not require dialogue removal or nonverbalizing every fact.|
|Scene material should not seize final directing/performing implementation.|Direct|Direct|Direct|Narrative action remains Scene Writer; final shots/acting remain future roles.|

## Complementary Candidates

|Source|Distinct contribution|Not supplied by the other two in the same form|
|---|---|---|
|BBC Writersroom|Screen-action observability, readable action beat, spec-script technical boundary.|Direct formal distinction between on-screen action and novelistic thought/backstory.|
|Academy Nicholl|Visual-literacy and prose-interiority-to-screen/performance translation; action/dialogue information allocation.|The adaptation comparison makes the inner-state conversion boundary explicit.|
|Writers Guild Foundation|Dialogue friction, contextual speech pressure, optional subtext techniques, clue-scaled exposition.|Direct scene-objective and opposed-aim questions plus limited subtext/exposition methods.|

## Overlap Candidates

- **Visible carrier versus exposition:** BBC/Academy require screen legibility; WGF explains why giving all information at once is not the answer.
- **Dialogue as action:** Academy asks what action versus speech communicates; WGF gives the direct competing-objective/friction check.
- **Collaborative space:** BBC and Academy restrict premature technical direction; WGF cautions against over-prescribing performance-style difference.

## Potential Tensions

|Tension|Evidence-bound reconciliation|
|---|---|
|BBC excludes superfluous novelistic thoughts/backstory from scene action; WGF allows indirectness and incomplete revelation.|Use screen-observable carriers while retaining inferential space. Do not turn either source into a universal “show everything” rule.|
|Academy treats film as visual; WGF treats dialogue as a vehicle of friction.|Visual storytelling does not equal silence. A line is retained when it changes the active relationship or carries needed conflict/information.|
|Format discipline versus creative/role flexibility.|Format rules are conditional on output context; Scene Writer must not use them to acquire Director/production authority.|
