# Continuity Display Prose Independence Contract V0.1

The same valid machine payload passes with:

- Chinese `受支持的变化`;
- different Chinese wording;
- English explanatory prose;
- minimum-sufficient explanatory text.

Machine validation consumes exact `primary_state_or_outcome` and structured State Evidence only. Prose is required to be non-empty explanatory evidence, but its vocabulary, language, and phrasing do not alter PASS/FAIL.
