# Continuity Signal Derivation Contract V0.1

There are no separate current integration fields named `presence` or `authorized_change`. Therefore no independent machine signals are derived from prose.

- Supported-change identity is the exact canonical outcome itself.
- Presence/location is a conditional, decision-relevant evidence dimension when supplied, not a universal output requirement.
- Future derived signals require an explicit authoritative structured field and deterministic rule; free prose remains ineligible.
